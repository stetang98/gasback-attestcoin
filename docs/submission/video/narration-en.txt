A failed transaction should not require a support ticket. GasBack lets a protocol sponsor a fixed rebate, paid only after the failure is independently verified.

The sponsor authorizes this exact transaction intent before the user acts. The ticket fixes who gets paid, the amount, and the claim window. This is a fixed sponsor subsidy, not a full gas refund.

Here is our actual Sepolia transaction. The demo deliberately reverts, so this is a reproducible failed receipt. The proof establishes the failure, not who was at fault.

After attestation, the relayer obtains a proof. Creditcoin verifies it through the native Attestcoin precompile. The vault then reads those same verified bytes and checks every sponsor policy condition. Attestation preparation time is shown separately.

The matching failed transaction receives the fixed sponsor rebate in test C T C. This is the mined claim receipt. A relayer may submit the proof, but cannot change the beneficiary or the amount.

Repeating this claim is rejected by a read-only call. Local policy tests also reject an altered payload and a successful source receipt. These checks do not represent extra mined transactions.

GasBack turns a remote failed receipt into a bounded, inspectable sponsorship decision. The source, proof, policy and payment evidence are available for review. All assets shown are test assets.
